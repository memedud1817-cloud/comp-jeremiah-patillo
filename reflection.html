Reflection and Process Record

This page explains how I worked on my assignments, what I learned, and how I put my portfolio together. Looking back at my work helped me see some of the things I improved on and some things I still need to work on.

Process Statement

Throughout this class, I worked on different types of writing. My first artifact focused on analyzing the poems by Richard Blanco and Langston Hughes. I had to look at how both authors used rivers to represent different things and explain what those meanings were.

The second artifact helped me look closer at my own writing. Instead of just putting a quotation into a paragraph, I had to explain why I chose it and how it supported my interpretation. This helped me understand that explaining evidence is just as important as finding it.

My third artifact was different because it focused more on technology and making an argument. I wrote about why companies should offer a free battery or essential component replacement during a device's lifespan. I also worked on a presentation about how AI could affect gaming console prices.

One of the biggest things I learned is that writing takes more than just getting your ideas onto paper. I have to organize my thoughts, explain my reasoning, and make sure the reader understands what I am trying to say.

Usability Notes on Content and Design

When putting together my website, I wanted it to be easy to navigate and have a design that matched my personality. I chose a black space theme with bright yellow stars and white glowing highlights. I wanted the website to look interesting without making the writing hard to read.

I organized the website into six pages so that each part of the portfolio would have its own place. The navigation bar allows someone to move between the Home page, Learning Claim, three Artifacts, and Reflection.

One thing I focused on was keeping the same design throughout the website. I wanted the pages to feel connected instead of looking like separate projects. I also used headings and sections to make longer assignments easier to follow.

Revision Log

Revisions to My Writing

  • In Artifacts 1 and 2, I added the clause "and how in its nature it is a beautiful part of the earth" to explain more about Blanco's river. This helped make my point clearer because I wanted to explain what the river represented before people changed it.
  • I also worked on explaining the quotation "thick to divide." Instead of only using the quotation, I explained how it connected to my interpretation of the river and its frustration.
  • Across my assignments, I tried to make my explanations clearer so that the reader would understand why I included certain examples and quotations.

Revisions to This Site

  • I organized the portfolio into separate pages so that the assignments would be easier to find.
  • I used the same navigation and visual style throughout the website to make everything feel connected.
  • I chose a dark background with yellow stars and white glowing details to make the website stand out while keeping the text readable.

Revisions Considered

I wanted to keep the website from becoming too crowded. Even though I wanted the design to have more detail, I also wanted the main focus to stay on my writing. I kept the layout organized so the reader could focus on each assignment without too many distractions.

AI Use Statement

I used AI only to help create the layout and design of my website, including the organization of the pages, styling, and overall appearance. The words, ideas, personal experiences, and writing in my assignments are my own. I used AI as a tool to help build and organize the website, not to write my essays or reflections for me.

What I Learned

Overall, this class helped me understand that writing is not just about having an opinion. I need to support what I say, explain my evidence, and think about how someone else might understand my argument.

I still have things I want to improve, especially grammar, organization, and explaining my ideas in more detail. However, I can see how my writing developed throughout these assignments. I want to continue getting better at expressing my thoughts and making my writing easier for other people to understand.
