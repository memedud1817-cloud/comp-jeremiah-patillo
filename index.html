Welcome to my e-portfolio for ENGL-110-19-F2F-FALL26. My name is Jeremiah Patillo. This portfolio has three pieces of my work from this class, and it shows how I started to understand the deeper meaning behind what I was writing about.

The first piece is about two poems by Richard Blanco and Langston Hughes. Both poems use rivers, but they use them in different ways. I also explain how I connect to the poems and what the James River in Richmond means to me. The second piece is about how my interpretation changed, how I revised one of my sentences, and why I chose a certain quotation. The third piece moves into technology. It includes my essay about free battery replacement and my presentation about how AI could affect gaming console prices.

I want this portfolio to show what I learned and how I got there. Start with the Learning Claim page, then look through the three artifacts. I included an introduction before each one so you know what I was trying to do. The Reflection page explains more about my process, but I left a few spots for information that needs to come from my own class experience.
